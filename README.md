Flutter Developer | UI/UX Designer | MIS & Software Development
---------------------------------------------------------------

I'm a passionate Flutter Developer and UI/UX Designer with a background in Management Information Systems (MIS), combining mobile development, user-centered design, and business understanding to create meaningful digital experiences.

I specialize in building responsive, scalable, and user-friendly cross-platform mobile applications using Flutter and Dart. My experience includes transforming Figma designs into functional interfaces, integrating RESTful APIs, managing application state with Riverpod, and developing multilingual applications with Arabic/English localization, RTL support, and light/dark themes.

Currently, I'm contributing to the development of WorkFlow, a SaaS project management platform, where I work on its mobile application and collaborate on product development and technical decisions. I've also served as the Lead UI/UX Designer and main Flutter Developer for MASAR, a humanitarian operations platform developed during TAQAT's CodeMap program.

I'm passionate about bridging the gap between design and development, solving real-world problems through technology, and continuously expanding my skills in mobile architecture, backend integration, and software engineering.

I enjoy collaborating with developers, designers, and innovators to turn ambitious ideas into practical, impactful products. 🚀
